# Invertebrate asset acquisition, 2026-10-30

Six model candidates are ready in `candidates.json`, with original and downstream provenance in `provenance.json`, extracted model-only downstream notices in `model-notices-excerpts.md`, and canonical CC BY4 text `CC-BY-4.0.txt` plus its official Creative Commons repository source/hash in `license-source.json`.

All six GLBs were downloaded as actual binaries from a public source repository, hash-matched to its specific model notices, and checked against the original per-model Sketchfab API metadata. The downstream licensing guide explicitly preserves CC BY4 for model derivatives/animations, separately from its NC backgrounds, audio and original editorial content. No code, narration, imagery or backgrounds were copied. Original source artist and downstream animation/preparation attribution are both included in each candidate metadata entry. Inputs end in `-museum.glb`; budgeted release candidates have plain species filenames. The source metadata and licensing snapshots remain in this evidence folder.

The source-preserving conversion script decodes meshopt where needed; welds/deduplicates; reduces geometry; resizes embedded images to512px; retains source skinning/materials/clips; and resamples animation. Anomalocaris and Mosasaurus require JPEG image encoding at quality82 to meet byte limits. All candidates are below20k scene triangles and2MiB, have embedded resources, and require no geometry decoder. Exact measurements and hashes are in JSON. `inspection.json` reports seven loaded entries and zero page errors, including research-only Dalmanites. `contact-sheet.png` was visually inspected and shows six recognizable reconstructions with a two-second Idle sample.
