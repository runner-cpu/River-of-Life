Notes are not persisted in Progress; permanent text beside the note remains visible after save and states that rerunning, refreshing or leaving loses the temporary note. The note belongs to the immutable result seed shown in its label, even when pending controls change. Saving freezes a temporary run-bound text snapshot, shown separately with textContent; further input edits leave that saved snapshot unchanged until another explicit save. A new completed run clears the snapshot and input. Browser checks verify saved/edited/rerun states and no Progress writes from unsaved editing. Only the badge ID and normal revision timestamp persist; no explanations, classifications, peer text or notes are stored. Repeated eligible actions do not rewrite the save or duplicate the badge; both badges award zero evolution points.
